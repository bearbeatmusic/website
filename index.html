Who we are

Built for artists who are almost there.

BearBeat Music is an independent incubator for musicians at the beginning of something. We're producers, engineers, and vocal coaches who work on the song and the artist behind it at the same time — because a great take means nothing if nobody knows whose it is.

01 / RECORD

Record

In-studio sessions with a certified vocal coach on every take, so the performance is right before it's ever mixed.

02 / DEVELOP

Develop

Sound, image, and story worked on alongside the music. We treat your brand as part of the production, not an afterthought.

03 / RELEASE

Release

Every song leaves the building fully mixed and mastered — ready to put your name on and put out into the world.

How it works

From vision to release.

Here's exactly what happens once you join the roster — start to finish.

01

Vision & Direction

We analyze your project and set a vision, understanding where it's going before anything gets recorded.

02

Sound Reference

We figure out what kind of songs fit your project and where it should sit sonically.

03

Songwriting Sessions

We write the song together and put together drafts, shaping it into something worth recording.

04

Sound Design

We explore and settle on the best sounds and textures for the track.

05

Vocal Recording

Vocals are recorded professionally in the studio, with a certified vocal coach in the room.

06

Review

We revise the song for any mistakes and schedule a re-recording session if anything needs another pass.

07

Mix & Master

The song goes to our mix and master engineer, with unlimited revisions until it's ready for platforms.

08

Social Setup

We link every social media account to your artist profile so everything points back to you.

09

Distribution

We distribute your song to every platform, 20 days ahead of release — enough time to run a real promotion campaign.

Everything through Sound Design can happen on-site or over a video call — whatever works best for you.
